## Key Takeaways from the Project

### Background

*Language experience* plays an important role in theories of online sentence processing. This study examined a battery of five tasks (the **fab 5**) designed to measure language experience:

1. Author Recognition Test
2. Extended Range Vocabulary Test
3. North American Adult Reading Test
4. Comparative Reading Habits
5. Reading Time Estimate

### Results

1. The fab 5 capture something about language experience.
2. The fab components are moderately related to each other.
3. Fab 5 measures relate to sentence comprehension and fixation latencies, accounting for other cognitive measures.
4. Condition effects as measures of individual differences are not necessarily reliable.
